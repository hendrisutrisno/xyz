# Physical demonstration

[Watch or download the video](physical_demonstration.mp4).

The 3 min 24 s silent video shows a fixed-base robot collecting objects, transporting stacks supported at their base, and delivering them. It illustrates the manipulation sequence; it does not measure execution-time savings or handling reliability. The setup is separate from the 40 synthetic computational instances.

Format: MP4, H.264, 1280 × 720, 24 frames/s, no audio. Source metadata was removed from the distribution copy.
